Pearlz Home Systems - Warranty Policy

1. Warranty Period

The standard warranty period for the AquaPearl 500 Pro is 24 months from the date of purchase (invoice date, not delivery date). The AquaPearl 300 carries a 12-month warranty.

2. Coverage

The warranty covers the purifier body, the internal pump, the motor, and all internal electronics against manufacturing defects.

3. Exclusions

The following are strictly excluded from warranty coverage:

Consumables such as filter cartridges and membranes.

Physical damage to the casing (e.g., dropping the unit).

Damage caused by poor water quality.

Unauthorised repair or opening of the unit by a local, non-Pearlz technician.

Commercial use of the home purifier.

4. Claim Procedure

To initiate a warranty claim, you must follow the official claim procedure detailed in the Service & Maintenance Agreement document. You will need your original digital invoice.
