Refund & Returns Policy

1. Return Window

Customers may return any unused Pearlz appliance within 14 days of delivery.

2. Condition Requirements

Unopened Items: Can be returned for a full refund. The customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

Opened/Used Items: We do not accept returns on items that have had water run through them due to hygiene standards, unless the item is defective upon arrival.

3. Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is inspected at our warehouse. Refunds are issued to the original payment method only. Promotional bundles must be returned in their entirety; partial returns of bundles will incur a restocking fee of 20%.
