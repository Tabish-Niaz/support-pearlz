Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I order a new filter?
A: You can order replacements directly from our mobile app or website.

Q: Can I return the machine if I don't like it?
A: Yes, within 14 days, provided it is completely unused and in original packaging. Please check the Refund Policy.

Q: Does the warranty cover the filters?
A: No, filters are consumables and are excluded from the standard 24-month warranty.

Q: How long does delivery take if I live in Karachi?
A: Next-day delivery is available for Karachi if you order before 2:00 PM.

Q: My machine keeps beeping three times and then stops. What should I do?
A: This means your pre-filter is saturated. You need to replace the filter cartridge and hold the reset button.
