Installation Guide

1. Pre-Installation Checklist

Ensure incoming water pressure is between 10-40 PSI.

Ensure a 220V power socket is available within 1.5 meters.

Minimum under-sink clearance of 60cm height is required.

2. First-Run Flushing Procedure

Before drinking water from a newly installed unit or after replacing the filters, you must perform a system flush. Let the water run continuously for 15 minutes to clear out carbon dust. Failure to do this may result in water that tastes weird or metallic.

3. Professional Installation

Professional installation can be booked for PKR 1,500. Self-installation is allowed, but any physical damage caused during self-installation will void the warranty.
