2. Technical Specifications

Flow Rate: 15 Liters / Hour

Tank Capacity: 8 Liters

Dimensions: 40cm x 25cm x 50cm

Power Consumption: 36W max

Filter Stages: 3-Stage Purification

3. Filter Maintenance

Filter cartridges are consumables. The sediment filter should be replaced every 6 months, and the RO membrane every 24 months.

4. Audible Alert Codes

E-01: Low water pressure. Check inlet valve.

E-02: Tank full, normal standby mode.

E-03: Three short beeps. Intermittent audible alarm indicating filter saturation (sediment pre-filter). Replace cartridge and hold RESET for 5 seconds.

E-04: UV Lamp failure. Contact service.

E-05: Leak detected in base. Unplug immediately.

E-08: TDS levels high.
