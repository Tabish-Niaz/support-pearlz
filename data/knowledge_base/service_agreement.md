Service & Maintenance Agreement

1. Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Tiers

Basic Tier: Includes 1 annual visit, free labor, parts billed at 10% discount.

Premium Tier: Includes 2 visits, free labor, and all consumable filters replaced for free.

2. Claim Procedure (Warranty & Service)

To submit a claim under warranty or your AMC:

Email support@pearlzhome.example with the subject "Claim - [Your Serial Number]".

Attach clear photos of the issue (e.g., damaged cabinet) and your digital invoice.

Our technical team will review the claim within 48 hours. If approved, a technician will be dispatched. Note: Property damage caused by leaks is not compensated under any agreement.
