Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

When you purchase a Pearlz product or use our mobile app, we collect your name, address, contact number, and payment details. The app also collects water-usage telemetry (liters purified per day, error codes generated) to provide timely maintenance alerts.

2. Data Sharing

We share your address and contact number with third-party courier services for delivery purposes, and payment details with our payment processor. We do not sell your data to advertisers.

3. Data Retention

Account data and telemetry are retained for as long as your account is active, and for 3 years thereafter for warranty and legal compliance.
