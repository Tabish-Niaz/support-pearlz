Shipping Policy

1. Delivery Timelines

Metro Areas (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad): Next-day delivery for orders placed before 2:00 PM.

Secondary Cities (Multan, Faisalabad, Peshawar): 3-4 working days.

Remote Areas (Skardu, Gilgit, Gwadar): 5-7 working days.

2. Shipping Costs

Shipping is free for all orders over PKR 10,000. For orders below this threshold, a flat rate of PKR 500 applies.

3. Exceptions

We do not currently offer express or next-day delivery outside of the designated Metro areas. International shipping is not available at this time.
