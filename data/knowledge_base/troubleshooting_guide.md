# Troubleshooting Guide

| Symptom | Probable Cause | Action / Remedy |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| No power to unit | Blown fuse or bad socket | Check socket with another appliance. |
| Leaking from base | Loose fitting or broken O-ring | Unplug immediately (Error E-05). Do not attempt to fix. Call support. |
| Water tastes weird | Carbon dust from new filter | Perform the First-Run Flushing Procedure. |
| Continuous running | Float valve stuck | Tap the side of the tank gently. |
| Three beeps | Pre-filter saturated (E-03) | Replace sediment filter and reset. |
| Blinking red LED | Pump overheating | Turn off for 30 minutes to cool down. |
| TDS reading high | RO membrane failed | Replace RO membrane (E-08). |
